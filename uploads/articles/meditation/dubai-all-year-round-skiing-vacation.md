Have you ever thought about snowing in the desert? Or, have you ever dreamed of skiing in the desert before? It may sound absurd and impossible, but in Dubai, everything is possible. From temperature controlled beaches, to man made islands, to seven star hotels, you will see that Dubai has everything that you will ever need to enjoy a fantastic vacation. So, what if you want to go on a skiing vacation? You will see that Dubai will be able to offer this to you.

So, just how can you go skiing in the middle of the desert? Well, in Dubai, you will see that they introduced the Alpine weather in the hot and arid desert through the Ski Dubai, which is in the Dubai Emirates Mall. Ski Dubai is one of the largest indoor ski slopes in the world which is able to produce real snow all year round. With an amazing 22,500 square meters of skiing area, you can be sure that you will be able to enjoy a great time skiing in the middle of the desert.

Basically, the indoor ski slope in Dubai is just one big and very efficient fridge. To produce the falling snow, pure water is put through a chiller and is sent through a series of pipes that leads to the snow guns which are located in the ceiling. When the chilled water is introduced in a freezing cold environment, it will turn in to crystals which make snow. This means that you will be able to experience winter all year round right in the middle of desert and really get real snow as if it came from nature.

Dubai is definitely a very unique place to go on a skiing vacation. Besides, in Dubai, you will be able to experience the best of both worlds. You will be able to enjoy going on a skiing holiday and even go to a climate controlled beach that will make you feel as if you are in a tropical country. And, because Ski Dubai is situated in the Dubai Emirates Mall, which is one of the largest malls in the world, you will be able to enjoy shopping after a day of skiing.

If you feel like taking going to the beach or a swim, then you will see that Dubai will be able to offer you pristine white sand beaches that are man-made as well as experience the thrill of the water parks has to offer in Dubai.
